# Pass 1 — Question (only)

For **INSERT TASK**, name the **one** engineering decision that is:

1. a **binary construction choice** (build A *or* build B — not “both,” not
   “phase 1 then 2,” not “configurable”),
2. where **A is maximum purity** (illegal states unconstructible; one authority
   per meaning; no spare-list of exceptions),
3. and **A is more falsifiable** than the safe choice **B** you are most
   tempted to pick because B is easier, greener sooner, or softer on you.

State that decision as a **single interrogative** that:

- names **A and B** explicitly,
- states **exactly how A is achieved** (types, membrane, register, gate —
  concrete mechanism, not aspiration),
- states **what becomes impossible** if A is chosen,
- and **closes every escape hatch**, including: deferral (“later story”),
  path/license carve-outs, soft-pass as design, baseline/grandfather, “good
  enough for now,” dual systems “until we migrate,” visitor/predicate
  bootstrap “then fix,” asking the operator instead of deciding, and narrative
  ADR prose that never pins a constructible law.

**Do not answer.** Do not list options. Do not recommend. Emit only that
question.
